# Phase 29 — Ads, creatives, insights, targeting and audiences migration

Phase 29 is `CODE_COMPLETE` for source and dependency-independent verification scope. Campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, synchronous/async insights and custom/lookalike/website-retargeting audiences now execute behind the unified MetaPlatform Business SDK transport and controlled cutover facades.

The migration preserves Phase 13 Ads mutation safeguards and adds independent exact approval, consent filtering, deterministic SHA-256 hashing and PII-safe audit behavior to every audience write. Direct audience rows require a consent grant plus at least one strong identifier (email, phone or external ID). The complete canonical hashed batch is locked by an explicit digest, and approval hashing covers the full sanitized payload without display-layer array/string truncation. Reads support logical legacy/shadow/platform selection with mode-aware bounded stale fallback; a mode change forces a provider attempt instead of serving a prior-mode cache as fresh. Writes support paused test-asset selection, explicit platform enable, independent legacy disable and emergency kill switches.
